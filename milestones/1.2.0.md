## Reliability
- Form storage is completed and read back before email is attempted.
- Email Notifications persists recipient-specific status and send intent under the script lock. Successful sends are not repeated for duplicate requests.
- Quota-limited mail stays Pending and the authorized five-minute worker retries it.
- Interrupted or uncertain sends become Needs review rather than automatically sending a possible duplicate. Check Sent mail before manually setting Pending.
- The worker repairs missing queue items for new email-era records. Historical requests are not emailed retrospectively.
- Sent means MailApp accepted the message, not proof of inbox delivery. Google sending quotas still apply.
